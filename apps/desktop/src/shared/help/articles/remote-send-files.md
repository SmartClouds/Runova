Send files lets you hand files to another computer the way TeamViewer and AnyDesk do: pick the files, pick the computer, and send. It works with your own computers and with the computers your teammates show to a team you share, at home, at the office or anywhere in between.

Both computers need Runova open and signed in. The files go straight from one computer to the other, encrypted the whole way. When the two can not reach each other directly they go through the Runova relay, which passes them on sealed and keeps nothing: not the files, not their names, and no record that anything was sent.

## Where to find it

Click **Remote** in the sidebar, then the **Send files** tab. You can also click the paper plane next to a computer on the **Anywhere** tab, or drop files on that computer's row there.

## Send files

1. Drop files or folders on **Drop files or folders here**, or click **Choose files** or **Choose folder**. A folder arrives as it is, with everything inside it. Add more at any time, or click the cross on a row to take it out.
2. Under **Send to**, pick a computer. Your own computers come first, then each teammate with theirs. A computer that is offline, or runs a Runova that is too old to take files, is listed but can not be picked, and says why.
3. Add a note if you like. The other person sees it with the files.
4. Click the button, which says what goes where, for example **Send 35 files to Office PC**. **Ctrl+Enter** does the same.

The send then shows under **Sent and received**, beside every tab of the Remote page, and in the status bar. It waits for the other person to answer for up to a minute, then moves the files, showing how far it is, how fast, and about how long is left.

## When someone sends you files

A window opens on top of everything, even with Runova minimized. It says who is sending, from which computer, what is coming with the size of each file and folder, and where it will be saved. Click **Change** to save these files somewhere else. Click **Accept** to take them or **Decline** to turn them away; saying nothing declines on its own after a minute. **Escape** declines too, and **Accept** is never the button a stray key press reaches.

When the files are in, Runova says so, and **Show in folder** opens the folder. Each file is checked against the sender's copy before it counts as received, so a file that did not arrive intact is never left looking saved.

## If the connection drops

A send that loses its connection says **Reconnecting** and picks up where it stopped once the two computers find each other again, for up to five minutes. Nothing is asked again and nothing is saved twice.

## Who can send you files

The **Receiving on this computer** card on the **Send files** tab sets this:

- **Who can send you files**: your own computers and your teammates, only your own computers, or no one.
- **Accept from my own devices without asking**: files you send yourself from another computer go straight in.
- **Save files to**: Downloads unless you choose another folder.

Teammates can send to a computer while it is shown to a team you share. Choose the teams under **Who can connect** on the **Anywhere** tab.
